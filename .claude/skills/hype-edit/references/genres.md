# Reading beats by content

The beats are the same everywhere; what counts as each one changes. Find the payoff first, then work outward from it.

## Shooter (GTA gunfights, any TPS/FPS)

- **payoff**: the kill that matters: headshot, multi-kill, clutch last enemy. Pin it to the frame the hit marker or blood appears; `focus` on the victim, `lock: true`.
- **hit**: shots exchanged, the player taking damage (`hurt`: in-game red flash, chromatic shimmer, health drop).
- **whip**: snapping in and out of cover, fast turns.
- **build**: aiming down sights while the target is still hidden; peeking.
- **lull**: reloading, crouched in cover.
- Stamp bank: HEADSHOT, ONE TAP, CLUTCH, DOUBLE KILL, NO SCOPE.
- Audio: shots show as sharp spikes in the envelope, but after demucs they are faint; trust the frames.

## Driving, stunts, chases

- **payoff**: the landing of a jump, the crash, the impossible gap, the near-miss at full speed. Pin it to the touchdown or contact frame; `focus` on the vehicle; `lock` only when it is a target you hit (a cop car, a rival).
- **hit**: bumps, scrapes, clipping traffic (`hurt` on a big crash of the player's car).
- **whip**: hard swerves, camera snaps, drifting into a turn.
- **build**: the straight run-up to the ramp, a long acceleration.
- **lull**: cruising after the landing.
- Stamp bank: SENT IT, CLEAN LANDING, NO BRAKES, TOO CLOSE, WRECKED.

## Sports and racing games

- **payoff**: the goal, the dunk, the finish-line overtake. `focus` on the ball or the car crossing; `lock` on the ball for a long shot.
- **hit**: tackles, blocks, contact.
- **build**: the run down the pitch, the final lap straight.
- Stamp bank: GOLAZO, GAME OVER, PHOTO FINISH, CALLED IT.

## Anything else

Ask what a viewer would rewind to see: that frame is the payoff. Whatever repeats on the way to it is a build; anything sharp that is not the payoff is a hit; anything after it with nothing happening is a lull. When a clip has no single moment worth slow-mo (a slow cinematic, a funny NPC), say so to the person instead of forcing a payoff; the library has no style for it yet, and adding one is an extension (`SKILL.md`).
